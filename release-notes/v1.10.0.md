# LunoPeak v1.10.0

OpenAI's GPT-5.6 family — Sol, Terra, and Luna — now shows up properly in your costs, along with a handful of other models LunoPeak had been guessing at.

## What's new

- **GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna are priced correctly.** LunoPeak didn't recognise the 5.6 slugs, so every Codex session on one was falling back to old GPT-5 rates. If you've been running Sol, your spend was being reported at roughly a third of what it actually cost. Those sessions now re-price themselves the next time LunoPeak reads them — expect your Codex numbers to jump, and to be right.

- **The July 30 price cut is respected.** OpenAI dropped Terra by 20% and Luna by 80% on the 30th. Sessions from before that date still cost what they cost, so your history stays honest instead of being quietly retconned to today's rates.

- **Several more models stopped being guesses.** GPT-5.4 nano was being billed at the older, much cheaper nano rate; the Codex-specific models (5.3, 5.2, and 5.1 Codex Max) and the Pro tiers had no entry at all and defaulted to GPT-5. All of them now have their own numbers.

- **The built-in assistant offers the 5.6 models.** Luna, Terra, and Sol are in the OpenAI model picker, with Sol as the new default. GPT-5.5 is still there; the 5.4 entries have been retired in favour of Luna and Terra, which are both newer and cheaper.

## One thing worth knowing

OpenAI charges a premium on single prompts over 272K tokens. LunoPeak doesn't apply it, because Codex only writes one running total per session rather than a breakdown per message — there's no way to tell a genuinely huge prompt from a long conversation that added up. Charging the premium on the total would inflate almost every long session, so a rare, very large prompt may read slightly under.

## Works on

- **macOS** 10.15+ (Apple Silicon and Intel)
- **Windows** 10+ (x64 and ARM64)
- **Linux** (x86_64)
