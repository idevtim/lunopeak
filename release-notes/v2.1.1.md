# LunoPeak v2.1.1

The window and the menu bar now open with your numbers almost right away, instead of showing loading placeholders for several seconds.

## What's new

- **Opening LunoPeak no longer re-reads all your history.** To work out costs, LunoPeak reads every Claude Code transcript on your machine. It used to forget that work every time it quit, so each launch started over and read every transcript again, one at a time. Now it keeps a summary of each session between launches and only reads the transcripts that changed since last time. On our own machine, with about 2.4 GB of history, the dashboard's stats and charts went from about 4.5 seconds to load to about 30 milliseconds.

- **The menu bar is quick on its first open too.** The menu bar runs separately from the main window, so it used to do that full read again on its own. It now shares the saved summaries with the window, so whichever one opens second doesn't repeat the work.

- **Your Claude limits stop re-reading the past week.** The cost and message count under your 5-hour and weekly Claude limits refresh every 30 to 60 seconds. Each refresh used to read every transcript from the last seven days — over a gigabyte for us. Now a refresh only reads transcripts that changed since the last one. Your Claude, Codex and Cursor limits are also fetched at the same time instead of one after another, so the limit cards fill in sooner when you first open the app.

- **The repo list loads faster.** LunoPeak now checks your repos side by side instead of one at a time. With 54 repos, that went from 1.7 seconds to under half a second.

Your numbers don't change. Costs read from the saved summaries are exactly the same as costs worked out from scratch.

## One thing worth knowing

The first launch after each update still reads everything once. LunoPeak deletes the saved summaries on every update, so that a fix to how sessions are priced always reaches your past sessions. On our history, that first read took a little under a second. Every launch after that is fast.

The summaries are saved in LunoPeak's settings folder, next to your settings. Each one holds a session's project, model, token counts, costs and opening prompt. That's information LunoPeak already showed you, taken from transcripts that were already on your disk. It doesn't leave your machine.

## Works on

- **macOS** 10.15+ (Apple Silicon and Intel)
- **Windows** 10+ (x64 and ARM64)
- **Linux** (x86_64)
