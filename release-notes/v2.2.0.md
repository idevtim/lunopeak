# LunoPeak v2.2.0

LunoPeak now knows Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2, so their sessions are priced at the right, lower rates and labelled by name.

## What's new

- **Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 are priced correctly.** Both cost $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output — 20% less than Fable 5.1. Cached reads cost $0.20 per million, and cache writes $10 (5-minute) or $16 (1-hour). LunoPeak didn't recognise either model yet, so it charged them at the Fable 5 rates: input and output read 25% high, and cached reads five times high. In a long agentic session, re-reading a cached prefix is often most of the bill, so the total could be well over the real cost. Sessions re-price themselves the next time LunoPeak reads them, and every session gets re-read once after you update. Expect your Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 numbers to come down.

- **Cache savings include the 5.2 models.** Like Fable 5.1, Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 charge for cached reads at a quarter of the usual tenth-of-input rate. The cache savings note in Costs now counts them: $0.60 saved per million cached tokens.

- **Sessions say "Fable 5.2" and "Mythos 5.2".** In the Sessions view, these sessions were labelled as plain Fable 5 or Mythos 5. They now show their real version.

- **Fable 5.2 is in the model picker.** You can pick it wherever LunoPeak asks for an Anthropic model. Mythos 5.2 stays out of the picker, like Mythos 5.1, because most accounts can't use it. It's still priced and tracked in Costs when you do use it.

## One thing worth knowing

Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 also have a fast mode, at $16 / $80 per million tokens. LunoPeak doesn't price fast mode yet, for these models or for Opus 5.5, so it prices every turn at the standard rate. If you use fast mode, those turns will read at about half of what they cost.

Your Fable limit already counts Fable 5.2 alongside Fable 5 and 5.1, so it works the same as before.

## Works on

- **macOS** 10.15+ (Apple Silicon and Intel)
- **Windows** 10+ (x64 and ARM64)
- **Linux** (x86_64)
