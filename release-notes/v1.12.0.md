# LunoPeak v1.12.0

LunoPeak has a new app icon. Same owl, same night sky — but on macOS 26 the system draws it rather than scaling a picture of it.

## What's new

- **The macOS icon is Liquid Glass.** The owl, the moon and the mountains are separate layers now, and macOS 26 renders them itself: the glass material, the specular highlight, the shadow and the rounded-square mask are all applied at draw time rather than baked into a bitmap. That's why it catches the light as it moves in the Dock, and why it holds up at every size instead of being one image resampled down to 16 pixels.

- **It follows your appearance setting.** Switch to Dark and the sky drops to near-black behind the same owl. The moon is the one thing held back from that: left alone, macOS recolours it to match the background and it goes navy, so it's pinned to the cream it has in Light. A moon that dims with the sky is just a smudge.

- **Older macOS gets the new artwork too.** On macOS 15 and earlier there's no live glass rendering, but the icon is a flattened render of exactly the same composition — so what's in your Dock changes either way. Nothing moved on the minimum version; LunoPeak still runs on 10.15.

- **Windows and Linux keep the icon they had.** Liquid Glass is a macOS feature and there's no honest equivalent elsewhere, so the flat illustration stays. If you run LunoPeak on more than one machine, the two will look different from here on. That's deliberate, not an oversight.

Nothing about tracking, pricing or your stored history changed in this release. Your numbers are exactly where you left them.

## One thing worth knowing

The glass icon isn't a pixel-for-pixel copy of the old one. The owl's chest feathers are gone and the moon sits a little further in from the corner — the first because fine linework turns to noise once the specular pass runs over it, the second because the system's rounded-square mask cuts deeper into the corners than the old artwork allowed for. Everything else is traced from the original, down to the ridgeline and the colour of the eyes.

## Works on

- **macOS** 10.15+ (Apple Silicon and Intel)
- **Windows** 10+ (x64 and ARM64)
- **Linux** (x86_64)
