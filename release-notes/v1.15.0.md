# LunoPeak v1.15.0

Anthropic released Claude Haiku 5.5, so LunoPeak now prices it, labels it and lets you pick it in the Assistant. While adding it, we checked every Claude model's price against Anthropic's current pricing page. Four models were being billed wrong. If you use Sonnet 5, your Costs page will drop.

## What's new

- **Haiku 5.5 is supported.** Its cost is worked out correctly in Costs, Sessions, the menu bar and the terminal status line, and Sessions labels it "Haiku 5.5". Haiku 5.5 is the first Claude model whose price depends on how long the prompt is. Prompts up to 100K tokens cost $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output tokens. A prompt over 100K costs five times that for the whole turn: $0.50 and $2.50. As with Claude Code itself, "the prompt" means everything sent: new input, cache reads and cache writes. Its context meter counts against a 1M window.

- **Sonnet 5.5 is supported too.** Sessions labels it "Sonnet 5.5". Haiku 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 are both in the Assistant's model picker now. The old "Haiku" entry is renamed "Haiku 4.5" so you can tell the two apart.

## Price fixes

- **Sonnet 5 stays at $2 / $10.** Anthropic launched Sonnet 5 with "introductory" pricing that was due to rise to $3 / $15 on September 1. Then they cancelled the increase. LunoPeak still applied it, so every Sonnet 5 turn since September 1 showed 50% more than you actually paid. Those turns now show the right amount. So do turns where LunoPeak couldn't tell the date.

- **Mythos 5.1 gets Fable 5.1's cheap cache reads.** When Mythos 5.1 came out, Anthropic hadn't said whether it shared Fable 5.1's $0.25-per-million cache-read rate. So LunoPeak charged it the full $1.00, to avoid showing a bill lower than the real one. Anthropic has now confirmed the $0.25 rate, so Mythos cache reads cost a quarter of what LunoPeak was showing. Mythos 5.1 also shows up in the Costs page's "saved" note, next to Fable 5.1 and Opus 5.5.

- **No more long-prompt surcharge on Opus 4.6, 4.7 and 4.8.** Anthropic now charges the normal rate across the whole 1M context window for these models. LunoPeak was still doubling the price of any prompt over 200K tokens. Opus sessions with long prompts now cost what they actually did.

- **Sonnet 4.6 context fills against 1M.** Its context meter assumed a 200K window until the session passed 200K. It now uses 1M from the start, like the other current models. With the Claude Code status line on, this was already exact.

## Fixes

- **The window opens where you left it.** LunoPeak used to re-centre its window every time it opened. Sometimes the centring went wrong and the window landed toward the bottom-right of the screen. Now it reopens at the size and position it had when you closed it, on macOS, Windows and Linux. If that spot is no longer on screen, say because you unplugged a monitor, it opens centred on your main display instead.

## One thing worth knowing

Anthropic's pricing page disagrees with itself about Sonnet 5.5 cache reads. The price table and the models overview say $0.20 per million. The prompt-caching section says $0.10. Until they agree, LunoPeak uses $0.20. If the real rate turns out to be $0.10, your Sonnet 5.5 cache reads will look a little more expensive than they were, never cheaper.

Costs are worked out again from your session files each time LunoPeak starts. Once you update, the fixed prices apply to all your past sessions as well as new ones.

These are list prices. LunoPeak doesn't account for fast mode, the Batch API discount, US-only inference or a negotiated enterprise rate. Fast mode costs more than LunoPeak shows. The others cost less or the same.

## Works on

- **macOS** 10.15+ (Apple Silicon and Intel)
- **Windows** 10+ (x64 and ARM64)
- **Linux** (x86_64)
