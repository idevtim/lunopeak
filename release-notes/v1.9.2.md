# LunoPeak v1.9.2

A correction to the usage tracking that shipped in 1.9.1 — it was checking your numbers with Anthropic far more often than it needed to.

## What's new

- **No more rate-limit warnings on your Claude usage.** 1.9.1 checked in often enough that Anthropic sometimes pushed back, and the percentages would freeze behind a "rate-limited" note for a stretch — the opposite of what that release was meant to fix. LunoPeak now checks on a gentler rhythm: your numbers still keep pace while you work, and they leave room for Claude Code's own checks, which draw on the same allowance.

- **The menu bar and the main window now share a single check.** They were each doing their own, quietly doubling how often LunoPeak asked. One check now serves both, so they also always show you the same figure.

- **A pause stays a pause.** If Anthropic does ask LunoPeak to wait, it now waits out the full period instead of occasionally starting up again early — so one pause no longer turns into a run of them.

## Works on

- **macOS** 10.15+ (Apple Silicon and Intel)
- **Windows** 10+ (x64 and ARM64)
- **Linux** (x86_64)
