# LunoPeak v1.9.1

A fix for Claude usage numbers that lagged behind what you'd actually used.

## What's new

- **Your Claude usage keeps pace again.** The 5-hour and weekly percentages could sit as much as fifteen minutes behind reality — long enough that a heavy stretch of work still looked light, and the number only caught up well after you'd moved on. It now tracks along with your session, so a glance at the menu bar tells you where you actually are.

- **Right again after your Mac wakes up.** Coming back from sleep used to leave the pre-sleep percentage sitting in the menu bar for a while. Waking now brings it current.

- **The menu bar and the dashboard agree.** The two kept their own separate idea of your usage and could briefly show different percentages for the same window. They now share one, so they always match.

## Works on

- **macOS** 10.15+ (Apple Silicon and Intel)
- **Windows** 10+ (x64 and ARM64)
- **Linux** (x86_64)
