# LunoPeak v1.11.0

Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 now have their own numbers — and because Fable 5.1 reads from its cache far more cheaply than Fable 5, its costs have been reading higher than you were actually paying.

## What's new

- **Fable 5.1's cheaper cache reads are accounted for.** Fable 5.1 bills cached tokens at $0.25 per million — a quarter of the Fable 5 rate, and half of Opus 5's. LunoPeak had been charging it the Fable 5 rate, so Fable 5.1 spend was reading four times high on that line. On a long agentic session, where most of the bill is re-reading a prefix that's already warm, that's often the largest part of the total. Those sessions re-price themselves the next time LunoPeak reads them — expect your Fable 5.1 numbers to come down.

- **Mythos 5 and 5.1 stopped being priced as Sonnet.** LunoPeak didn't recognise the Mythos names at all, so every Mythos session quietly fell back to Sonnet rates and reported roughly a third of what it actually cost. They now price at the Fable tier they belong to. If you're in Project Glasswing, expect those numbers to go up, and to be right.

- **Costs shows you what the cheaper rate saved.** Any model carrying a discounted cache-read rate now gets a small marker in Cost by model, with a line underneath totalling what it saved and across how many cached tokens. It's money already reflected in the figures above it — not something still waiting for you to act on.

- **Fable 5.1 is in the assistant's model picker,** next to Fable 5. Sessions labels it properly now too, along with the Mythos models.

## One thing worth knowing

Anthropic lists Mythos 5.1 as sharing Fable 5.1's pricing, but hasn't confirmed it gets the same cheaper cache reads. LunoPeak charges Mythos 5.1 the standard rate rather than assume a discount that might not be there — better a budget alert fires slightly early than slightly late. Everything else about its pricing is exact, and if that rate is confirmed, this becomes a one-line change.

Mythos 5.1 is deliberately absent from the assistant's model picker, since it's reachable only through Project Glasswing and would fail for most keys. It's fully tracked in Costs either way.

## Works on

- **macOS** 10.15+ (Apple Silicon and Intel)
- **Windows** 10+ (x64 and ARM64)
- **Linux** (x86_64)
