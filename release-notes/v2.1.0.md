# LunoPeak v2.1.0

Your Claude costs now include the work your subagents do. Until now, every session that handed work to a subagent was reading low.

## What's new

- **Subagent spend counts toward the session that started it.** When Claude Code hands a task to a subagent, the subagent runs in its own conversation and writes its own transcript. The main conversation only gets the final answer back. LunoPeak only read the main conversation, so everything a subagent spent was missing from your totals. Now each subagent's tokens and cost are added to the session that started it, on the day they were spent. The Costs page, today's total in the menu bar and terminal status line, the Sessions list and Session Replay all include it.

- **Your past numbers go up, and that's the fix.** This applies to your whole history, not just new sessions. The first time LunoPeak reads your transcripts after updating, any session that used subagents is priced again with their spend included. The gap depends on how much you delegate. In our own sessions that used subagents, the typical one was missing about a sixth of its tokens, one in four was missing more than 30%, and one that delegated nearly everything was missing 85%. A session that never used a subagent doesn't change.

- **You can see the subagent share.** On the Costs page, the "All time" card says how much of your total subagents spent. In Session Replay, the cost shows how many subagents ran and what they cost. In the Sessions list, hover over a cost to see the same breakdown.

- **The detail line under your Claude limits is more complete.** The cost and message count under your 5-hour and weekly Claude limits now include subagent runs, since they count against the same limits. The percentages come straight from Anthropic and are unchanged. Only LunoPeak's own numbers beside them go up.

## One thing worth knowing

Only spend rolls up. A session's message count, tool calls and edited files still describe the main conversation, so a session's shape still matches the conversation you had. A subagent that ran forty tool calls adds to the cost, but not to the session's step count.

## Works on

- **macOS** 10.15+ (Apple Silicon and Intel)
- **Windows** 10+ (x64 and ARM64)
- **Linux** (x86_64)
